Candela Bible Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

Candela Bible ("the app") is a Bible reading app published by GEM CREATORS. This policy describes the app as built for version 1.0.0.

Summary

The app works offline. We do not collect your personal data, we have no servers that receive it, and the app needs no account. What you enter stays on your device unless you choose to share or export it.

Information we collect

None. The Android app does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send data over the network itself. It contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting software, and no sign-in.

Information stored on your device

The app keeps these in its private storage on your device:

  • Highlights, notes and bookmarks
  • Prayer list entries, including any names you type and answered-prayer notes
  • Reading history, plans and plan progress, streak and activity days with time zone, points and achievements
  • Memory verses, quiz scores and recent passages
  • Settings, such as language, theme, text size and reminder time
  • The verse of the day and streak shown by the home-screen widget

We cannot see or access any of this.

Backups

Device cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for this app, so your data is not copied to your Google account by the app's backup settings. A backup exists only if you create one. It is an unencrypted JSON file containing the data listed above, including notes and prayers. You choose where it goes, so please keep it somewhere you trust.

Sharing and other apps

Some actions hand information to another app or service, only when you start them:

  • Share, copy and verse images: the content goes to the app you choose, or to your clipboard.
  • Export and import a backup: the file goes to, or comes from, the location you choose.
  • Listen: Bible text is passed to the text-to-speech engine on your device. That engine is separate from this app and has its own privacy terms.
  • Links in the About screen: these open in your browser, where that website's privacy policy applies.

Google Play or the App Store may give us aggregate statistics about installs and app performance under their own terms, and we do not receive your in-app content from them.

Permissions (Android)

  • Notifications: daily reminders and the playback control for reading aloud. Optional.
  • Run at startup: restores your reminders after the device restarts.
  • Foreground service, media playback: continues reading aloud with the screen off.
  • Wake lock and network state: requested by a system library used for the home-screen widget. The app does not use them to send data.

Deleting your data

Clear the app's storage or uninstall the app to delete everything it holds on your device. Any backup files you made are kept until you delete them yourself.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version at this address and update the effective date.
